FOR MAGNET CONTROLLERS AND AC AND DC TYPE M AND MX CONTACTORS Copper contact tips are standard. Silver-faced contact tips are available and are recommended for applications where the contactors remain closed for long periods of time. Silver-faced contact tips are standard on crane manual-magnetic disconnect switches and are optional on DC starters. Each kit contains movable and stationary contacts, necessary hardware and servicing instructions. ▲ Each kit contains complete set of parts to change contacts on both “Lift” and “Drop” contactors.
